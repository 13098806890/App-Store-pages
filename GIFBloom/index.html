GIFBloom · iOS app

Make your moments move.

Turn videos and Live Photos into animated cutouts, then compose them into layered scenes with text, effects, and sound.

Create and edit animated scenes

Extract people from selected videos and Live Photos on your device. Layer multiple elements, adjust their position, size, rotation, and timing, then add backgrounds, frames, stickers, text, and sound.

Export your creation

Place extracted audio on a timeline, then export as GIF, transparent HEVC video, or H.264 video. GIFBloom requires iOS 17 or later. Your media stays on your device; GIFBloom does not upload your photos or videos.

GIFBloom · iOS app

Make iPhone stickers from videos.

Turn a person in a video or Live Photo into a custom sticker. Refine its edge, save a transparent PNG to Photos, then add it to your iPhone sticker collection.

1. Cut out a person

Choose a video or Live Photo and refine the subject cutout and sticker edge. Person cutout requires iOS 18 or later.

2. Save your sticker

Export the sticker as a transparent PNG to your Photos library. Your media stays on your device; GIFBloom does not upload your photos or videos.

3. Add it to iPhone Stickers

In Photos, touch and hold the saved subject, tap Add Sticker, then use it in Messages and other supported apps. GIFBloom also lets you compose animated scenes from your media.
